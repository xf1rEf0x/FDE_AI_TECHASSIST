# TechAssist AI – Building an Intelligent IT Support Assistant

Your team at GlobalLogic has been hired by TechAssist Solutions to build an AI-powered IT
Support Assistant for employees, IT support engineers, and system administrators.
The client doesn't want a finished product immediately. They want an MVP that grows over
multiple phases. Your job is to implement those phases while adapting to new business
requirements. The same application evolves across multiple levels.
